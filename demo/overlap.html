OVER-REPORT RATE -
the fraction of union hits the exact ribbon test rejects, at this angle and speed, over a seeded batch through the real sweptOverlap / sweptOverlapExact. Axis-aligned reads 0% (the ribbon is the union box); push to 45° and fast and it climbs past 50% — the bench worst cell measured 54.8%.
EXACT ⊂ UNION -
every exact hit is a union hit, checked pair-for-pair over 0 seeded pairs across all angles. One exact hit without a union hit would light this red — the strict-subset guarantee, on stage.
